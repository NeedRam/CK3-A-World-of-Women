The relay saves 0xd0 bytes, so the sixth callback argument points at relay RSP +0x238. The callback reads that slot only for immediate origin 0x2de53f1 and preserves the requested gender for wrapper origin 0x2eaba44.

The profile verifies the unique wrapper-frame context, custom-ruler registration context, and Designer-to-wrapper call target.
